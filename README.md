## 📚 Module Overview

### 1. [Foundations & Preliminaries](./01-foundations/)
* Basic mathematical logic and set theory
* Real numbers ($\mathbb{R}$), completeness axiom, supremum, infimum, maximum, and minimum
* Mathematical Induction Principle
* Function properties (injective, surjective, bijective, composite, inverse)

### 2. [Sequences of Real Numbers](./02-sequences/)
* Formal limit definition ($n \to \infty$) and convergence
* Squeeze Theorem, Sign Permanence, and Monotone Convergence Theorem
* Asymptotic growth hierarchy (infinites) and indeterminate forms
* Euler's number $e$

### 3. [Limits of Functions & Continuity](./03-limits-and-continuity/)
* $\varepsilon - \delta$ formal definition and one-sided limits
* Standard/notable limits and asymptotic equivalents
* Discontinuity classification (removable, jump, essential)
* Weierstrass, Intermediate Value, and Zero-Point Theorems

### 4. [Differential Calculus](./04-differential-calculus/)
* Derivatives, physical/geometric meaning, and differentiation rules
* Rolle, Lagrange (Mean Value), Cauchy, and L'Hôpital Theorems
* Full curve sketching (monotonicity, extrema, concavity, inflection, asymptotes)
* Taylor and Maclaurin expansions (Peano and Lagrange remainder)

### 5. [Integral Calculus](./05-integral-calculus/)
* Indefinite integration: Parts, Substitution, Partial Fractions
* Definite Riemann integral and Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
* Improper/generalized integrals and convergence criteria

### 6. [Numerical Series](./06-numerical-series/)
* Infinite series, partial sums, and the $a_n \to 0$ necessity test
* Geometric, Harmonic, and p-series
* Positive-term series tests (Comparison, Ratio, Root, Integral)
* Alternating series, Leibniz Criterion, and Absolute vs. Conditional convergence

### 7. [Complex Numbers](./07-complex-numbers/)
* Algebraic ($a + ib$), polar, and exponential ($re^{i\theta}$) forms
* Basic arithmetic, modulus, conjugate, and argument
* De Moivre's theorem and complex roots

### 8. [Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs)](./08-differential-equations/)
* Initial Value Problems (Cauchy Problem)
* First-order separable and linear ODEs
* Second-order linear ODEs with constant coefficients

### 9. [Useful Algebraic Methods & Extra Tools](./09-extra-methods/)
* **Polynomial Factorization:** Ruffini's synthetic division, quadratic factoring, completing the square
* **Algebraic Tricks:** Conjugate rationalization, strategic $+1/-1$ terms
* **Prerequisites:** Trigonometric identities, logarithm/exponential rules, inequalities
