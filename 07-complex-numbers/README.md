# 07. Complex Numbers

Algebraic and geometric properties of complex numbers ($\mathbb{C}$).

---

## 📌 Key Topics & Representations

### 1. Representations
* **Algebraic Form:** $z = a + ib \quad (i^2 = -1)$
* **Polar/Trigonometric Form:** $z = r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$
* **Exponential Form:** $z = r e^{i\theta}$

### 2. Modulus & Conjugate
* **Conjugate:** $\bar{z} = a - ib$
* **Modulus:** $|z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{z \bar{z}}$

### 3. De Moivre's Formula & Roots
$$z^n = r^n (\cos(n\theta) + i\sin(n\theta))$$
$$w_k = \sqrt[n]{r} \left( \cos\left(\frac{\theta + 2k\pi}{n}\right) + i\sin\left(\frac{\theta + 2k\pi}{n}\right) \right), \quad k = 0, 1, \dots, n-1$$
